# Data attribution and redistribution

`data/raw/ONET/` contains unmodified official downloads. `data/normalized/ONET/` contains source-preserving JSONL representations created by World Classification Atlas: format conversion, explicit provenance fields, and reconstruction of the content model's parent links from its element codes. Original labels and supplied descriptions are retained. No missing source definitions are generated.

This repository includes information from the [O*NET 31.0 Database](https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html) by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA), used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA. World Classification Atlas has modified the representation of this information. USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed, or tested these modifications.

The separately stored [O*NET-SOC 2019 CTDL occupation collection](https://www.onetcenter.org/ctdl/graph/ce-8350d6ad-bc2a-11ef-8f5a-005056bdd76e) is an official download linked from the database page. It identifies its own modification date and is not relabeled as the database 31.0 edition. The [database content license](https://www.onetcenter.org/license_db.html) covers downloadable database files linked from that page; it does not automatically cover the entire O*NET website, tools, or unrelated crosswalks.

Other standards retain their respective owners' rights. Registry entries, factual discovery metadata and checksums are published as research records. Full UN source structures, correspondence workbook, local normalized outputs and full evidence pages are excluded from Git under `data/local/`; their presence on disk does not establish permission for public redistribution. Do not remove that exclusion without reviewing each resource's rights. No copyrighted commercial standard has been copied into the public data directories.

ISIC economic-activity research publishes selected factual code-pair reviews, stable Atlas IDs, source-code bindings, GSIM identifiers, clause offsets/hashes and file/row/page references. The current directory includes 107 Rev.5 class-scope anchors, 23 Atlas-authored bounded activity nodes and 118 navigation ancestors. Atlas activity labels, bounded scope statements, review rationales and IDs are project-created research, not official publisher definitions or subcodes. The six accepted research supplements and two held candidates are explicitly separate from the official 605-row correspondence table.

Official labels, definitions, complete correspondence rows/change descriptions, quoted evidence spans, workbooks, manuals and full enriched source representations remain local-only. Public clause selectors contain locations and checksums rather than the selected source text. The research index does not grant a license to redistribute those source materials and is not a complete unified classification.
