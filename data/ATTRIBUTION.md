# Data attribution and redistribution

`data/raw/ONET/` contains unmodified official downloads. `data/normalized/ONET/` contains source-preserving JSONL representations created by World Classification Atlas: format conversion, explicit provenance fields, and reconstruction of the content model's parent links from its element codes. Original labels and supplied descriptions are retained. No missing source definitions are generated.

This repository includes information from the [O*NET 31.0 Database](https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html) by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA), used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA. World Classification Atlas has modified the representation of this information. USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed, or tested these modifications.

The separately stored [O*NET-SOC 2019 CTDL occupation collection](https://www.onetcenter.org/ctdl/graph/ce-8350d6ad-bc2a-11ef-8f5a-005056bdd76e) is an official download linked from the database page. It identifies its own modification date and is not relabeled as the database 31.0 edition. The [database content license](https://www.onetcenter.org/license_db.html) covers downloadable database files linked from that page; it does not automatically cover the entire O*NET website, tools, or unrelated crosswalks.

Other standards retain their respective owners' rights. Registry entries, factual discovery metadata and checksums are published as research records. Full UN source structures, correspondence workbook, local normalized outputs and full evidence pages are excluded from Git under `data/local/`; their presence on disk does not establish permission for public redistribution. Do not remove that exclusion without reviewing each resource's rights. No copyrighted commercial standard has been copied into the public data directories.

ISIC economic-activity research publishes selected factual code-pair reviews, stable Atlas IDs, source-code bindings, GSIM identifiers, clause offsets/hashes and file/row/page references. The current directory includes 110 Rev.5 class-scope anchors, 30 Atlas-authored activity nodes and 123 navigation ancestors. Three mask activities have current-edition evidence only; candidate old-source bindings and a held directory proposal are separate from approved relations. Atlas activity labels, bounded scope statements, review rationales and IDs are project-created research, not official publisher definitions or subcodes. The six accepted research supplements and two held candidates are explicitly separate from the official 605-row correspondence table.

Selected CPC2.1 product-to-ISIC4 industry-of-origin code facts are auxiliary evidence, with explicit citations and no claim of semantic equivalence. CPC products are not merged into the economic-activity directory. The original CPC manual, corrected workbook, correction notice, selected product notes and complete source workbook remain local-only.

Official labels, definitions, complete correspondence rows/change descriptions, quoted evidence spans, workbooks, manuals and full enriched source representations remain local-only. Public clause selectors contain locations and checksums rather than the selected source text. The research index does not grant a license to redistribute those source materials and is not a complete unified classification.

## Eurostat / NACE furniture research

Source: European Commission, Eurostat, *NACE Rev. 2.1 – Statistical classification of economic activities in the European Union*, Publications Office of the European Union, 2026, [DOI 10.2785/3034037](https://data.europa.eu/doi/10.2785/3034037). © European Union, 2026. The text is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (manual physical page 4); its third-party cover image is excluded. Complete manual PDFs remain local.

`data/raw/NACE/` preserves the unmodified official ShowVoc notes workbook, correspondence workbook, download inventory and Eurostat reuse-policy HTML acquired on 2026-11-08 UTC. The linked dissemination components carry upload timestamps of 2026-09-30 (notes) and 2026-09-29 (correspondence). These are not new NACE classification revisions. Source: [Eurostat NACE](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/nace/), [official correspondence entry](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/nace/correspondence-tables), [Eurostat reuse policy](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/help/copyright-notice). Reuse of Eurostat metadata and dissemination tools is authorised with acknowledgement, subject to individual exceptions. No third-party images are extracted or republished.

`data/normalized/NACE/` converts the original workbooks to JSONL, retains original cells, and derives parents from the official Section/Level/Code columns. No missing definitions are supplied. Atlas Chinese activity labels, bounded scopes, selected paths and research relations are project-authored modifications, not official Eurostat translations or correspondence approvals. Eurostat is not responsible for these modifications. The NACE branch has one current furniture class covering several material scopes; it is not declared equal to the two ISIC Rev.5 furniture classes. UNSD full correspondence and definitions remain local under the existing restriction above.

The 2026-11-09 follow-up includes Eurostat Cellar SPARQL result snapshots and the official NACE Rev.2→Rev.2.1 workbook. The exact read-only query texts are in `data/queries/`; JSONL conversions preserve RDF URI/literal types, language, official note fields and original result rows. These Eurostat metadata are reused under the same attribution/change-notice policy above. The public ShowVoc project catalogue contains metadata about other publishers and therefore remains local-only pending a separate redistribution review.

NACE Rev.2 RDF notes are a separately identified dissemination component, alongside the preserved 2008 manual. The official NACE2–ISIC4 XKOS associations and their publisher `closeMatch` predicates are not rewritten as Atlas equivalence. The group46.6 discrepancy with the UNSD table is retained. New furniture process activities, a current-only parts scope and separate repair/installation boundary nodes are Atlas research; group-level evidence and historical gaps are visible. The old ceramic-furniture candidate has an explicit bounded-allocation decision and retained history; it does not establish whole-class equivalence or remove the sanitary-fixture conflict.

The 11 March 2024 UNSD explanatory notes are a separate local-only snapshot. The furniture directory publishes its checksum, selected clause offsets and research decisions. Source quotations, original note revisions and underlying publisher rows for the material comparisons are available in the locally rebuilt evidence dossier. The CPC 2.1 furniture-parts reference records a product-to-principal-industry citation, with a pending ISIC Rev.4 activity binding kept separately. [2024 UNSD explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/ISIC5_Exp_Notes_11Mar2024.pdf)

Selected historical activity-index references are from the Office for National Statistics, UK SIC 2007, Crown copyright, under the Open Government Licence v3.0 except where otherwise stated. The original PDF and September 2024 reformatted workbook are kept locally. The public index records publisher codes, selector locations and Atlas decisions. [ONS index releases](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/classificationsandstandards/ukstandardindustrialclassificationofeconomicactivities/uksic2007) · [ONS terms](https://www.ons.gov.uk/help/terms-conditions)

The CPA 2008 service clauses are from the European Commission, Eurostat. The local dossier retains the original service excerpts. The furniture activity directory records their evidence locations. The CPC 2.1 manufacturing-service reference is an activity-of-origin citation alongside that distinct product evidence.
